Our Story

Why AdmitEdge Exists

We started AdmitEdge because we saw too many brilliant students rejected — not for lack of talent, but for lack of strategic guidance.

In 2014, our founders — a former Yale admissions officer and a former Stanford admissions reader — noticed a pattern. The students who got into top universities weren't always the ones with the highest GPAs or test scores. They were the ones who understood how to tell a compelling story about who they were and what they cared about.

Meanwhile, thousands of equally brilliant students were being rejected because no one had taught them how to translate their achievements into a narrative that admissions committees could remember. The playing field wasn't level — and it had nothing to do with intelligence.

AdmitEdge was built to fix that. We bring former admissions officers directly into the student's corner, giving every family access to the same strategic insight that elite private schools and legacy networks have always had.

Today, we've mentored over 1,850 students from 40+ countries — and we're just getting started.

Our Values

What We Stand For

Four principles guide every engagement, every essay review, and every counselor match we make.

Ethical Counseling

We never write essays for students. Every sentence must be theirs. Our role is to teach, guide, and edit — not to fabricate.

Authentic Narrative

We help students discover and amplify their true voice — not invent a persona. Admissions committees can spot authenticity instantly.

Equal Access

Every student deserves access to expert guidance — regardless of zip code, school type, or family connections.

Measurable Outcomes

We track every outcome and publish our results. 96.8% of our students are admitted to at least one of their top three choices.
